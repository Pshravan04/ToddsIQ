Frequently asked questions

It’s fun, safe, and creatively empowering. Follow these simple tips to make the most out of your ToddsIQ™ Drawing Robot experience.

What age group is ToddsIQ™ suitable for?

ToddsIQ™ is designed for children aged 3 and up, making it the perfect creative companion for young artists.

Is ToddsIQ™ screen-free?

Yes, ToddsIQ™ is 100% screen-free, allowing your child to focus on their creativity and imagination without distractions from electronic devices.

How does ToddsIQ™ help my child improve their drawing skills?

ToddsIQ™ uses smart technology to guide your child step-by-step through each drawing, helping them build artistic skills and confidence while encouraging creativity and imagination.

What comes in the ToddsIQ™ bundle?

The ToddsIQ™ bundle includes the ToddsIQ™ Drawing Robotand a full set of guided drawing cardsto help your child draw with ease, sparking their creativity every day.

Is ToddsIQ™ rechargeable?

Yes! ToddsIQ™ is rechargeable and portable, making it convenient for use at home or on the go. Simply charge it and let the creativity flow!

What if my child doesn’t like ToddsIQ™?

We are confident your child will love ToddsIQ™. However, if for any reason they’re not happy with their purchase, we offer 30-days money-back guarantee, no questions asked!

What’s included in the ToddsIQ™ Drawing Robot bundle?

The ToddsIQ™ Drawing Robot bundle includes the following:

  • 1 ToddsIQ™ Drawing Robot
  • 150 Drawing Cards to inspire endless creativity
  • 2 Markers for drawing
  • 1 Charging Cable for easy recharging of the robot

Everything you need to start creating and having fun is included in the bundle!
